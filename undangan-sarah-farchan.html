Our Love Story

2024

A Gentle Beginning

Our paths first crossed beneath the same roof, in the middle of ordinary days neither of us had ever planned for. Back then, we were simply two people learning how to stand again, carrying our own unfinished stories, quietly trying to begin a new chapter in life. Yet somehow, among all the small coincidences, the simplest conversations slowly became the safest place to return to. The days that once felt ordinary turned into something we began to look forward to without realizing. And before we knew it, two souls who were once searching for direction found each other instead.

2024 – 2026

Weaving the Story

Ours was never a love that grew under perfect circumstances. Not every journey begins in ways that are easy to understand. Time has its own way of revealing meanings, softening uncertainties, and leading hearts exactly where they are meant to be. And somewhere in the middle of all the becoming, we discovered a kind of peace we had never known before. We learned that love is not merely about finding someone who makes life beautiful, but finding someone who chooses to stay even when life is not.

June, 2026

Becoming Home

And one day, among the prayers we whispered quietly to the sky, conversations about the future no longer sounded like distant wishes. They became certainty. And that certainty finally led us here. With the blessings of Allah Subhanahu wa Ta'ala, we choose to begin a new chapter as two souls learning to forgive, to celebrate, to grow together, and to chase new dreams side by side. We are ready to find laughter even in imperfect days, and to keep choosing the same way home, again and again, for the rest of our lives.
